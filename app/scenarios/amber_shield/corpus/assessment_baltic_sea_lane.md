Assessment: throughput of the Baltic sea line of communication under the mine and coastal-missile threat.
1. Date. 08 September 2026.
2. Key judgement. There is a roughly even chance that the Baltic sea line of communication sustains 30 escorted transits per day through the Danish Straits into Klaipeda and Gdansk. Analytic confidence is high, because the clearance rate and the threat inventory are both well established.
3. Klaipeda. The Klaipeda sea port of debarkation is vulnerable. The approach channel has not been swept since the fleet sortie of 08 September 2026 and there is no alternate berth for roll-on roll-off shipping.
4. Gdansk. The Gdansk sea port of debarkation is postured to react, with two sweep teams and a shore-based escort cell held at short notice.
5. Mine countermeasures. The mine countermeasures group has 6 vessels in the Baltic, of which four are available for the clearance task on the northern route.
6. Implication. The fifth planning assumption is the least supported of the eight and drives the sea port selection decision and the escort allocation.
