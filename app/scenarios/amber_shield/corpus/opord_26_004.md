Operation order 26-004, Operation Amber Shield, headquarters United States European Command.
1. Date. 09 September 2026.
2. Situation. Russian forces seized the Narva crossings and Ida-Viru County on 05 September 2026 and hold blocking positions in eastern Latgale. Forces in Kaliningrad Oblast and in Belarus threaten the Suwalki corridor. The North Atlantic Council invoked Article 5 on 06 September 2026. C-Day is 10 September 2026 and D-Day is on order, notionally thirty days after C-Day.
3. Mission. United States European Command, as the supported combatant command, deploys, receives and employs designated forces in the Baltic Region under North Atlantic Treaty Organization command, defends Allied territory, airspace and sea lines of communication, and on order conducts and enables Alliance counteroffensive operations to expel Russian forces from Estonia and Latvia, in order to restore the territorial integrity of Estonia and Latvia and preserve the credibility of Article 5.
4. End state. Russian forces are expelled from Estonia and Latvia. United States European Command and Allied forces are likely to control Latgale and the international border at the close of Phase III. The Suwalki corridor, the Baltic sea ports of debarkation and the Danish Straits are open, and escalation has been contained below the nuclear threshold.
5. Planning assumptions. Eight planning assumptions are carried in the assumption table of the decision matrix. They cover Alliance consensus, host-nation transit and basing, the limits of Russian long-range strike, sealift and prepositioned stock sufficiency, the Baltic sea line of communication, nuclear signalling, Belarusian commitment and the mission partner network. Each is validated or invalidated as reporting arrives.
6. Priority intelligence requirements. Seven requirements are established and are tied to the decision points of each course of action.
7. Sustainment. Reception, staging, onward movement and integration through Poland and Germany; prepositioned stock draw at Powidz.
8. Command and signal. United States European Command retains combatant command; authority transfers to Alliance command at closure; the mission partner network provides interoperability for United States forces.
