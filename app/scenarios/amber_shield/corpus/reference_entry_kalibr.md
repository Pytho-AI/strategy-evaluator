Reference entry: Russian long-range strike systems bearing on the Baltic Region.
1. Date. 04 September 2026.
2. Kalibr land-attack cruise missile. The sea-launched land-attack variant is carried by Baltic Fleet corvettes and by Northern Fleet submarines. From the launch baskets Russia holds today, the maximum effective range of the Kalibr land-attack cruise missile against Allied territory is likely 2500 kilometres. Analytic confidence is high. That reach covers the whole of the reception corridor through Poland and Germany and does not reach the continental United States.
3. S-400 surface-to-air missile system. The longest-range interceptor of the S-400 surface-to-air missile system reaches 400 kilometres and anchors the air defence umbrella over Kaliningrad Oblast and the eastern Baltic.
4. Iskander-M missile system. The Iskander-M missile system is dual capable and is held in Kaliningrad Oblast by the 152nd Guards Missile Brigade. Its conventional variant ranges the reception nodes in north-eastern Poland.
5. Implication for planning. The third planning assumption of the operation order rests on the reach stated at paragraph 2. It assumes away a capability Russia holds rather than one it lacks, and nothing the command does changes the decision.
