# Style guide: guidance documents (doc_type `guidance`)

Sources: CJCSI 3100.01F Encl. A §3.c(2)–(3), Encl. C §1–2 (NSS, NDS, NMS), Encl. D §3–6 (JSCP,
GCP, CCP); JP 5-0 Glossary (contingency plan, OPORD). Fictional content, real product types and
issuing roles. Directive paragraph scheme; <= 400 words.

    1. <Product type spelled out> (<acronym>) <fictional title>, <DD Month YYYY>.
    a. Issuing authority. <role: President / SecWar / CJCS / CCDR>. Parent guidance: <title>.
    b. Strategic direction. Two to four sentences of fictional guidance.
    c. Objectives. (1) Objective <id>: <statement>. (2) ...
    d. Nesting. This product implements <parent> and directs <children>.
    e. Assumptions provided to subordinate planning. (1) <assumption statement> (higher-headquarters assumption).

Real CCMD names appear only as organizational roles ("the supported combatant command"); never as
owners of fictional facts. Adversaries are the fictional actor names only.
