# Style guide: news (doc_type `news`)

Narrative, dateline `<Place>, DD Month YYYY —`, quoted officials by role, sometimes vague
("roughly 150 miles", "sources say"). May carry `hedge_drift`, `unit_drift`, `paraphrase`,
`stale_echo` (quoting an older report with its own date), `distractor` sentences outside the
predicate vocabulary, `alias`. Not an intelligence product: no `[src:]` brackets required, but the
markings, acronym rules and date format still apply.
