# Style guide: assessment (doc_type `assessment`)

Two sub-kinds share the doc type:

1. Intelligence assessment (author: J-2 role). ICD 203 vocabulary only (`icd203.md`); every fact
   sourced; confidence in separate sentences.
2. Risk assessment for one harmful event (author: J-5 role). JRAM vocabulary only
   (`jram_risk_statement.md`): the Fig. 11 risk statement on its own line, the drivers with
   their kinds and locus (internal/external), sources of risk (threat/hazard), consequence basis
   (Fig. 23 strategic value x degree of damage, or the Fig. 28 row and cell), trend, and the
   opportunity statement (Fig. 12) when a beneficial counterpart exists. If the forced-choice rule
   fired, a paragraph "Posture factors" lists the friendly posture factors that drove the plot.

A single product never contains both vocabularies (invariant 13), except the one deliberate
`mixed_scale` negative example, which the style check must flag.
